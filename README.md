# MaggieMix 🥘

**180+ Pakistani, Indian & South Asian home recipes — bilingual (Urdu + English), built for my mom.**

Live at **[maggiemix.com](https://maggiemix.com)**.

MaggieMix exists to answer the eternal question: *"aaj kya banayein?"* (what should we cook today?). Karahi, biryani, nihari, chaat, mithai, chai. Filter by how much effort you have left in you, or hit **Pick For Me** and let the app decide dinner.

## Features

- **180+ curated recipes** from genuine desi home cooks
- **Bilingual** — Urdu and English
- **Filter by effort and type** — quick weeknight daal vs. weekend nihari
- **Pick For Me** — one tap picks dinner for you
- **PWA** — installable on a phone home screen, works like an app

## Tech

A deliberately simple, dependency-free single-page app: hand-written HTML/CSS/JS with a PWA manifest. No framework, no build step — open `index.html` and it runs.

Part of the projects at [annasrahman.com](https://annasrahman.com).
